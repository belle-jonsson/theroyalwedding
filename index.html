san miguel de allende

San Miguel de Allende is a city that enchants everyone who visits — with its rose-pink cathedral, vibrant streets, and golden light, it's pure magic and we can't think of a better place to celebrate love.

travel & accommodation

We want the journey to be just as enjoyable as the destination, so we've done a little of the legwork for you. Below you'll find our curated recommendations on flights, transport, and accommodation to help make your way to San Miguel as seamless as possible.

flights & airports

QRO - Recommended

Querétaro International Airport

The closest airport to San Miguel, roughly 1 hour by car. Smaller and calmer than Mexico City, QRO is served by a growing number of direct routes.

Easy to navigate and quick to get out of. This is the smoothest arrival experience.

BJX

Bajío International Airport (León/Guanajuato)

Also very close and well-connected, particularly useful if you find better flight options from your city (e.g. San Jose, Oakland). It's a comparable drive to San Miguel — around 1.5 hours — another easy, stress-free airport to arrive into.

MEX

Mexico City International Airport (Benito Juárez)

The largest hub with the most international connections, especially useful for guests coming from Australia, the UK, and other long-haul origins — or anyone who wants to tack on a few days in one of our favorite cities!

Give yourself at least 24 hours in CDMX — it's a world-class city and absolutely worth it.

Stay in Condesa or Roma Norte! 3.5 hour drive to San Miguel.

what's your best option?

From the East Coast

Most guests flying from the East Coast will find the best connections through Houston, Dallas, Atlanta, Detroit or Mexico City.

From the West Coast

Direct connections from Los Angeles to QRO and San Jose / Oakland to BJX via Volaris, VivaAerobus, and Aeromexico. MEX is also well-served.

From Australia & The UK

Mexico City is your friend! MEX is the natural hub for long-haul international flights, and it's a wonderful reason to spend a day or two exploring the capital. We recommend you take the first bus or shuttle of the morning to beat the traffic.

transport & transfers

Once you land, getting to San Miguel is straightforward. We recommend booking transfers in advance, especially around the wedding weekend when demand is high.

Private Shuttle — BajioGo (Recommended)

The most trusted and well-reviewed shuttle company in the region, run by a local San Miguel team. They serve all three airports — QRO, BJX, and MEX — and have been doing this for over ten years. Bilingual drivers, door-to-door service, a sign with your name in arrivals. They're prompt, professional, and genuinely lovely. Book via WhatsApp or their website well in advance. We used them on our trips!

Please use discount code GeorgiaJackWedding when booking to receive a discount.

Private Car Transfer

If you're travelling in a group of 2 or more, a private car sometimes works out to be better value than individual shuttle seats. Several reliable operators run this route. Heros Transportation is well-regarded for the QRO run; or your hotel concierge can usually arrange a private car from any airport.

First Class Bus (from Mexico City)

If you're coming from Mexico City, ETN and Primera Plus run comfortable, reliable first class buses between the city's Central del Norte bus terminal and San Miguel de Allende. It's a pleasant ride — reclining seats, air conditioning, and great scenery. Again: take the earliest departure to avoid traffic crawling out of the capital.

accommodation

San Miguel is full of extraordinary places to stay. We've pulled together a range from iconic five-star properties to intimate boutiques, so there's something for every style and budget. We'd encourage you to book as early as possible; the best rooms go quickly, especially around event weekends.

faq

What is the dress code?

Formal attire is requested — dresses for women and suits for men. One practical note: San Miguel's cobblestone streets are beautiful but uneven, so block heels or wedges are your best friend.

Can I bring my child?

We love your little ones, but our wedding will be an adults-only celebration.

What will the weather be like?

April in San Miguel is about as good as weather gets. Warm, golden days around 85°F (30°C), crisp and starlit evenings around 52°F (11°C), and blue skies with barely a cloud in sight. Pack a light wrap for the evening and your sunglasses for everything else. The wedding will be outdoors.

Can I bring a plus one?

Our guest list is lovingly but firmly set! Official invitations will be on their way later this year, and plus ones will be named on the invite itself. If they're not listed, we unfortunately aren't able to accommodate them.

Is Uber available?

Uber works at all the airports, in San Miguel and in Mexico City. It is a reasonable option if you're travelling solo and light.

Can I eat the street food?

Yes, and you absolutely should — Mexican street food is one of life's great pleasures. The city is well-established as a tourist destination and food hygiene standards are generally very good. The usual common-sense rules apply: look for busy stalls with high turnover, stick to freshly cooked food, and avoid anything that's been sitting out. We recommend Andy’s Taco Cart! Do NOT drink the tap water, however.

How do I pronounce San Miguel de Allende?

sahn mee-GEHL deh ah-YEHN-deh :)

Do I need a car in San Miguel?

Almost certainly not. The historic centro is wonderfully walkable — most of what you'll want to do, eat, and see is within a 15-minute stroll. Street parking is extremely limited and the cobblestone streets are best navigated on foot.
